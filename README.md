# NSE Business Growth & Volume Dashboard

This project automates an NSE Business Growth & Volume dashboard using Python, SQLite, and Streamlit.

The dashboard tracks monthly and quarterly business growth across 4 NSE market segments:

1. Capital Market
2. Equity Derivatives
3. Currency Derivatives
4. Interest Rate Derivatives
